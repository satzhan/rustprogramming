Strong balanced 2-in / 2-out randomizer

Roster connection randomizer

Paste a roster, then generate two peer connections for every student. The generator keeps every student at exactly two outgoing and two incoming connections whenever the class size allows it.

One student per line is best. Commas also work. Duplicate names are automatically numbered.

What makes this stronger than the circle version?

Instead of putting students in one shuffled circle and assigning the next two neighbors, this builds two independent balanced layers. Each layer gives every student one target and makes every student receive once. Then it performs random edge swaps that preserve the exact incoming/outgoing counts.

So the final result is still mathematically balanced, but the connections are scattered rather than looking like a seating-circle pattern.
